SmartChoice HVAC

Comfort when you need it.

SmartChoice HVAC is the customer-facing HVAC service operated by Rolling Stone Technology, LLC.

SmartChoice HVAC provides customer service-request coordination for heating and cooling service, estimates, tune-ups, diagnosis, and repair.

Service request communications

Customers who submit a service request may receive transactional communications related to that request, including request confirmations, appointment scheduling and confirmations, appointment reminders, technician updates, and service-status notifications.

SMS consent

Customers may choose whether to receive transactional SMS messages. The SMS consent checkbox is not preselected.

SMS notice: Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.

For service requests, customers may provide their contact information, service address, requested service, equipment type, and preferred appointment dates and times.
