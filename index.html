Service request communications

Customers who submit a service request may receive transactional communications related to that request, including request confirmations, appointment scheduling, appointment reminders, technician updates, and service-status notifications.

SMS notice: Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase.

For service requests, customers provide their contact information, service address, requested service, equipment type, and preferred appointment dates and times.
