Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

BrightBlue Comfort HVAC collects information that customers provide when requesting service, including name, email address, telephone number, service address, requested service, equipment information, and preferred appointment dates and times.

We use this information to process service requests, communicate about scheduling and service status, provide requested services, maintain business records, and protect our systems.

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are used for service-related communications. We do not sell mobile information or SMS opt-in consent to third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.

We may use service providers to deliver communications and operate supporting systems. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to protect legal rights.

Customers may reply STOP to an SMS to opt out of future SMS messages and HELP for assistance.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.
