Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

SmartChoice HVAC is the customer-facing HVAC service operated by Rolling Stone Technology, LLC. This Privacy Policy describes how SmartChoice HVAC and Rolling Stone Technology, LLC collect and use information provided in connection with HVAC service requests and related communications.

Information we collect

We may collect information that customers provide when requesting service, including name, email address, telephone or mobile number, service address, requested service, equipment information, and preferred appointment dates and times.

How we use information

We use this information to process service requests, communicate about scheduling and service status, provide requested services, maintain business records, and protect our systems.

Mobile information and SMS consent

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are used for service-related transactional communications. Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Customers who opt in may receive messages concerning service-request confirmations, appointment scheduling and confirmations, appointment reminders, technician updates, and service-status notifications. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of future SMS messages and HELP for help. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchase.

Service providers and legal disclosures

We may use service providers to deliver communications and operate supporting systems as necessary to provide the requested services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect legal rights.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.
